# Secondary Contact figures

`task2_resolution` shows individual matched-chromosome scores, paired score
differences with bootstrap intervals across centered, varying-demography, and
broad-resolution gates, plus parameter-wise natural-scale errors.

`task2_correction` shows paired untouched evaluation scores, parameter-resolved
error reduction, and transfer under six generator perturbations. The extreme
asymmetric-migration limitation is retained and visually distinguished.

The PDFs are vector masters; PNGs are 360 DPI review copies. Colors are
colorblind-safe, structure is redundantly encoded with lines and marker shapes,
and all text uses normal font weight.
