# Figure specification and review

The image was generated with the built-in image generation tool and checked against the submitted task methods. It follows the user's annual-review figures: a white background, blue panel letters, thin pale-blue dividers, blue/teal/lavender illustrations, compact typography and task-specific rows.

The final composition has four panels:

- A: Tasks 1–4, demographic inference from frequency spectra, including projections, model families, optimizer starts and selection rules.
- B: Tasks 5–8, selective-sweep localization, including window sizes, step sizes, score thresholds, consensus procedures and interval widths.
- C: Tasks 9–12, relatedness inference, including exact or detected IBD, feature summaries, classifier types, pedigree structure and background correction. Task 12 ends with a pair-classification table.
- D: Tasks 13–14, low-coverage inference through ANGSD, winsfs and moments, including read-quality filters, block/window settings, convergence tolerances, epoch limits and optimizer starts.

The final edit removed both bottom footer statements. Visual review checked all fourteen task rows, panel order, legibility and the corrected Task 12 output illustration. The publication asset is `ghist_methods.png` (1536 × 1024 pixels).
